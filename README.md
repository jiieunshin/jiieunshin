### About Me
I studied **statistical learning** with a focus on variable selection at the University of Seoul and earned a Ph.D. in Statistics. In addition, I have conducted research on nonparametric inference, the application of generalized linear models, and model selection. Currently, I work as a researcher in the **Biostatistics** division at the National Cancer Center(NCC) and am interested in developing statistical models applicable to real-world. I am currently conducting research on the estimation of treatment effects based on causal inference models and the analysis of healthcare big data.
