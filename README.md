### About Me

I received a Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Seoul, where my research focused on statistical learning and variable selection. My academic work has also included nonparametric inference, generalized linear models, and model selection.

Currently, I am a researcher in the Biostatistics Division at the National Cancer Center (NCC), where I develop statistical methodologies and models for real-world applications in healthcare and medicine. I pursue research grounded in practical needs from the field, with particular interests in causal treatment effect estimation and the analysis of healthcare big data.
